Throw a pot.

Your clay has just been slapped onto the wheel, a little off-centre. Pick a hand, start the wheel, and hold the left mouse button to touch the clay. Where the cursor sits on the clay is where your hand pushes.

  1. Centre. Space starts the wheel. With Palms 1, squeeze the sides until the wobble is gone. Squeezing cones it up, Press 2 spreads it down again.
  2. Open. Thumb 3: press down at the very centre, stop above the wheel to leave a floor, then drag outward to widen it.
  3. Pull. Pull 4: put the cursor on the wall and lift slowly. The wall gets thinner and taller. [ ] set the pinch.
  4. Shape. Finger 5 pushes in, the inside finger of Pull pushes out, the Rib 6 straightens. Needle 7 levels a ragged rim.
  5. Export. Preview a glaze, then download an STL in millimetres, sealed and ready to slice.

Clay is never created or lost: squeeze it and it rises, press it and it spreads. On a still wheel your hand only dents one side. Hands dry the clay; sponge it with W. Right-drag or Shift-drag to look around, scroll to zoom, Z to undo.
